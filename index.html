The internet made appreciation easy. It did not make it economic.

People already signal value every day with likes, reactions, shares, and comments. But those actions rarely translate into direct financial support. Existing payment systems are usually built for larger transactions, subscriptions, or donations. PennyPay is designed to fill the gap between a free reaction and a major purchase.

PennyPay’s core asset: protected platform architecture.
